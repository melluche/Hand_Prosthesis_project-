## Goals

Design and program a humanoid robotic prosthesis (hand + forearm), 3D-printed, capable of:
- Gripping movements of the fingers (flexion/extension), ring and littre fingers are controlled by the same servomotor
- Rotating the wrist around its own axis, 
- Optionally, being controlled remotely
- Make the hand **lighter** than typical solutions, since weight and load imbalance are major issues for people with amputations
- Keep the design **maintainable** (replaceable parts: palm, fingers, etc.)
- Keep the development **eco-responsible** and the model **open-source**
